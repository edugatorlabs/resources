### 1.1 Key Features

Edugator provides everything you need to learn and practice coding effectively:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Problem Description** | View the current task and requirements in the left panel |
| **Navigation** | Switch between problems using the top center buttons |
| **Code Editor** | Write and test your solutions in the right panel |
| **Submit Code** | Run your solution against automated tests to verify correctness |
| **Run Code** | Execute your code with sample input for quick testing |
| **Submission History** | View your score and submission history in the top-left corner above problem title |
| **AI Teaching Assistant (Bloom)** | Get help with reasoning, debugging, and understanding errors |

<br>

### 1.2 About Bloom - Your AI Teaching Assistant 😊

The integrated chatbot is designed to guide your learning 💡:

- ✅ Helps you understand concepts and debug errors
- ✅ Has access to your code to provide personalized assistance
- ✅ Guides you toward solutions without giving direct answers
- ❌ Does **not** have access to problem descriptions (to encourage independent reading and develop prompting skills)

## 🚀 2. Getting Started

### Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Navigate to the sign-up page: **https://edugator.app/sign-up**
2. Register using:
   - **Email:** Your university email address
   - **Password:** Choose a secure password


### Step 2: Join Your Course

1. Check your email for a course invitation
2. Select the course you've been invited to
3. Click **"Accept"** to join

### 3.2 Common Questions

**Q: Can I use any programming language?**  
A: Check with your instructor about supported languages for your course.

**Q: How many times can I submit?**  
A: You can typically submit unlimited times. Each submission will be recorded in your history and the last submission is used for grading.

**Q: Will Bloom give me the answer?**  
A: No, Bloom is designed to help you learn by guiding you toward the solution, not providing it directly.
